Game design doc · concept · Oct 2026

Night
Shift

Keep your patients alive. The ones you lose come back for you.

Online co-op · 2–6 players Top-down 2D Godot 4 → Steam

01The game

By day, an understaffed hospital admits patients and restocks, and you decide how many patients to risk taking on. By night, something is wrong with the patients. You treat them with real multi-step protocols against a ticking timer. The ones you fail flatline and get back up as the turned.

Saved patients pay out insurance money, which buys barricades and weapons to hold the hallways against the turned and against hordes from outside. Survive until the 7am shift change, collect rewards from the patients who made it, then do it again.

02One round

One day plus one night is a round. Day is calm and about decisions. Night is when everything happens.

03How it fits together

TreatSave a patient and get paid
↓ money
BuildBuy barricades, ammo, supplies
↓ walls hold the hallways
FightHold off the horde with weapons
↓ every second fighting is a second not treating
TurnUntreated patients rise inside your walls, and the horde grows

Five rules that tie it together

  1. Hallways do double duty. Barricades steer the horde, but they also block your own gurneys.
  2. Two horde sources. Waves hit the doors from outside. Patients you fail rise behind your walls, so turtling doesn't work.
  3. Saved patients fight back. A discharged patient grabs a mop and defends for 60 seconds.
  4. The horde breaks things. Lose the pharmacy and you have no meds until it's fixed.
  5. Screamers speed up dying. Every patient near one deteriorates faster.

04Treatments

Each condition is a short protocol the doctors write. You carry one thing at a time, and a gurney takes two people.

Broken armX-ray → splint from supply closet → cast station
OverdoseNarcan → monitor → don't leave them alone

05Roles

Pick any role. Two doctors or three security guards is fine. Everyone can do every task, and your role just makes you much better at one thing.

RoleBetter atSignature moveEveryone else
DoctorDiagnose fast, always rightRevive one flatlining patient"WebMD": 3x slower, wrong 1 in 4 times
NurseCarry 2 items, fast medsSees nearby patient timersTimers only on the board
Security2x damagePlaces barricadesCan shove
MaintenanceFast builds and repairsRestores power instantlyRepairs at 1/4 speed

The timer board is a spot on the map. Whoever stands there sees every patient's timer and calls out who's dying.

06Admissions

Every day, patient cards queue at the admissions desk. Each patient brings in money, and each one could turn at night.

ConditionChest pain · 3 steps RiskHigh Payout$400 JobElectrician

Beds are your limit. Admit past it and patients go on hallway gurneys, right in the horde's path and in the way of your own routes.

Every day comes down to: "The electrician is high-risk and we're out of beds. Hallway gurney?"

06bWhen a patient dies

Every death, from any cause, costs the same five things:

#PenaltyWhen
1No payout. Patients only pay when dischargedInstant
2Their job perk is goneInstant
3They turn, unless you act in 10 seconds+10s
4The family sues: a fine × the cause multiplierMorning
5Your rating drops, so tomorrow's patients are worseMorning

The 10-second window

  • Code them A Doctor revives them or uses the defib paddles. They're back and every penalty is cancelled.
  • Morgue run Two people wheel the body to the morgue. They don't turn, but you still pay the fine.
  • Do nothing They get up inside your walls, usually next to other patients.

How bad the fine is

CauseFineWhy
Nobody treated them1xNeglect
The horde got them0.5xInsurance covers half
Died on a hallway gurney1.5xYou chose to overbook
Wrong treatment2xActual malpractice
Friendly fire3xPlus an incident report with your name on it

Contagion: a turned patient who bites a living one turns them too. One neglected bed can take out a whole ward in about 30 seconds.

How you lose

  • The ICU is overrun: the night is lost.
  • Your rating hits zero stars: the state shuts the hospital down and the run is over.

Fines can put you in debt, but debt only locks the shop. You never lose beds or roles, so a bad night is always recoverable. The morning report lists every death: "Bed 3: chest pain, WebMD misdiagnosis, −$800, nurse: Andy."

07Emblems

09Weapons

Everything is improvised from the hospital. You have one hands slot (an item or a weapon) and a belt slot for a small melee weapon. Guns take both hands, so holding a gun means you're not carrying meds. Dart-gun ammo is the same sedative stock your patients need.

10First playable

About 4–5 weekends. The smallest version that tells us whether it's fun:

  • 2 players online, 1 small hospital (3 beds, pharmacy, ICU, 2 hallways)
  • Doctor + Security, 3 conditions written by Navo and dcai
  • Barricades, an IV pole and a dart gun
  • Shamblers outside, turned patients inside
  • One day (5 patient cards, a 3-item shop) and one 5-minute night

It answers two questions: does a barricade blocking your own gurney feel tense, and is a 4th patient on a hallway gurney tempting enough to argue about?

Done means: two people on different computers play a full night, save at least one patient, fight a wave, and someone laughs. dyang plays it with no instructions beyond "you're a doctor, save people."

Technical details (for the devs)
EngineGodot 4, GDScript
ViewTop-down 2D, PointLight2D + shadows for flashlights
NetworkingGodot high-level multiplayer (MultiplayerSynchronizer, MultiplayerSpawner, RPCs)
TransportENet in dev, Steam Networking (GodotSteam) in release
Host modelListen server, no dedicated servers, $0 infra
VoiceSteam voice → AudioStreamPlayer2D (post-MVP)
Repo / CIGitHub + Git LFS; godot-ci export → steam-deploy
  1. The host is authoritative. Clients send inputs.
  2. Own movement predicted locally. Grab/treat/build confirmed by the host, first request wins.
  3. Enemies follow a shared flow field to the ICU. The host sends spawns + 10–20Hz snapshots, and clients interpolate.
  4. No networked rigidbody physics. Gurneys and items are kinematic.
  5. Hits are detected on the attacker's client, confirmed by the host. Projectiles are host-simulated.
  6. Test with 2 local instances. Use Steam app ID 480 until the $100 Steam Direct fee.

Conditions, enemies, roles, emblems and night timelines are .tres Resources, so the doctors can add a case without touching code. Emblems subscribe to game events (on_admit, on_patient_death, on_enemy_killed, …) on a global event bus.

Out of scope for the first playable: local co-op (dropped, online only), Steam integration, voice, Nurse/Maintenance, power cuts, the other 4 enemies, perks, emblems, rating, the cure, night modifiers, 3D art.
